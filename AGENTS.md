# Klassenklar Project Guidelines & Memory

## Project Overview
Interactive learning and exam preparation application for the official German Meat Classification Exam (**Fleischklassifizierung**).

## Reference Source
- Reference PDF: `C:\Users\klajd\Downloads\Klassifizierung_Notizen.pdf` (contains all mandatory topics to pass the classification exam).

## Core Exam Topics
1. **ZP-Verfahren (Zwei-Punkte-Messung)**:
   - Specific anatomical landmarks between 2nd and 3rd to last rib.
   - Speckmaß (S) and Fleischmaß (F) measurements.
   - Mathematical formula for lean meat percentage (Magerfleischanteil MFA).
2. **Classification Devices & Technologies**:
   - **Auto-FOM I & III**: High-speed ultrasound array, Splitbox, Strömungswächter, transducer checks, maintenance and calibration seals.
   - **Hand-FOM**: Optical probe, depth measurement, calibration blocks.
   - **Optigrade MCP**: Optical reflection sensor.
   - **Visual Grading**: Subjective classification reserved for Zuchtschweine / Sauen.
3. **Slaughtering & Cutting Guidelines (Schnittführung)**:
   - Permissible and impermissible cuts.
   - Defect categorization and standard correction factors (Korrekturfaktoren: Hirn, Augen, Ohren, Flomen, etc.).
4. **Legal, Metrology & Protocols**:
   - **Eichamt & Instandsetzerzeichen**: Calibration requirements, legal seals, annual checks, calibration certificates.
   - **Verwiegung**: Weighing timeline (within 45 minutes of slaughter), valid weight intervals (50.0–119.9 kg).
   - **Protokolle**: Wiegeprotokoll & Klassifizierungsprotokoll requirements, 12-month legal archiving mandate.
   - **Meldepflicht**: Legal price reporting (Preismeldung) thresholds.
5. **Handelsklassen**:
   - Official classes: S (>= 60%), E (55-59.9%), U (50-54.9%), R (45-49.9%), O (40-44.9%), P (< 40%).
   - Separate categories for Zuchtschweine (M, V).
